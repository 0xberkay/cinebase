FAQ

Questions & answers

Does CINEBASE upload my photos?

No. CINEBASE runs 100% on-device. Your photos never leave your phone — all AI processing happens locally, with no internet connection required.

What is the maximum output resolution?

CINEBASE can render masters up to 8K (7680×5120 pixels) depending on the source image and your device capabilities.

What film looks are available?

14 cinematic grades: Kodak 2383, Teal/Orange, Noir, Golden, Neon, Sepia, Faded, Cool, Vivid, Mono, Cyberpunk and more — applied instantly with one tap.

What export formats are supported?

Save to your photo library as JPEG, or export a lossless TIFF master for professional use. Share directly via the system share sheet.

What is the difference between Standard and AI Enhance?

Standard mode preserves every original pixel while upscaling. AI Enhance mode lets the AI reimagine and rebuild fine detail — ideal for very low-resolution or damaged photos.
